# The North Texas Starter Map (McKinney & Collin County)

This is a researched head start for Weeks 2–5, verified against live sources in
August 2026. It is a **starting map, not the finished census** — Week 3's job is
to expand it, confirm what's currently posted, and add the smaller shops no list
ever captures. Openings and programs change; treat every entry as "verified
recently, re-check before acting."

The headline: you live in one of the best places in America to run this exact
plan. A major defense electronics campus is *in McKinney*, the largest wire and
cable plant in the country is *in McKinney*, and Texas Instruments is building a
$30 billion semiconductor campus 35 minutes up US-75 that needs thousands of
technicians — with a community college pipeline built specifically to feed it.

## The education picture (this is unusually good)

- **Collin College** (campuses in McKinney, Plano, Frisco, Wylie) — the obvious
  Week 5 starting point:
  - A dedicated **Engineering Transfer** program (calculus/physics/intro
    engineering sequence built for transfer into BS Engineering programs)
  - The **Academic Alliance** with **UT Dallas** and **Texas State**, including
    guaranteed-transfer pathways and co-enrollment — get the engineering course
    plan in writing from an advisor (acadalliance@collin.edu, 972-599-3167)
  - The **Technical Campus in Allen** (~10 min away): advanced manufacturing,
    welding, HVAC, robotics — the credential-fast routes
- **UT Dallas** (Richardson, ~25 min) — the natural ABET-accredited transfer
  target for EE; commutable from home
- **Grayson College** (Sherman/Denison) — the TI semiconductor-technician
  pipeline; pairs perfectly with a Sherman fab job
- **UNT (Denton) and UT Arlington** — additional in-region ABET engineering
  schools if the UTD math changes

## The electrician route, locally

**IBEW Local 20 / North Texas Electrical Training Center (NTX JATC):**
applications accepted **year-round** ($20 fee), apprentices start around
**$20.33/hr plus ~$2.91/hr into insurance & retirement** with scheduled raises,
and classroom work earns college credit through Dallas College. Local 20's
office is in Grand Prairie (972-263-1122); training center info at ntejatc.org.
For a McKinney EE-leaning kid who wants to earn from day one, this is the
strongest no-college-first option in the region.
